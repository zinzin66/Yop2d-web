Complete games made with Yop2D, the free no-code 2D game engine for Android. Open them in Yop2D ("Examples" tab of the start screen), play them, then look at their nodes and change them to learn.

Firefly Night

A choice story (visual novel) with Hana, Yuki and Rin: the game asks your name, then petals at sunset, a firefly forest, a thunderstorm with lightning, a timed choice, magic snow in the middle of summer… and 4 endings. All made with nodes.

⬇️ Download the project (3.6 MB)

Nexus UI

An interface showcase in hologram style: animated menu, sliding panels, switches that really change the game, volume, a shop with coins, an energy bar that recharges and a reward window. All made with nodes.

⬇️ Download the project (929 KB)

Memory cards

A memory game: flip 2 cards at a time and find the 8 pairs of little creatures in as few moves as possible. The cards change places every game.

⬇️ Download the project (286 KB)

Dungeon escape

An isometric dungeon escape game: search the room, break pots, crack the chest code and find 3 gold coins to buy the key from the orc. With an inventory, a timer and texts in 9 languages.

⬇️ Download the project (423 KB)

Dungeon walk

Walk around an isometric dungeon with the joystick: the hero goes behind the column or in front of the table, bumps into furniture and changes image with his direction. Find the 5 hidden gold coins to open the gate. Shows draw order by position and iso-view walking.

⬇️ Download the project (535 KB)

Climb & run

The character changes animation by itself: walk, duck, jump and climb ladders (seen from behind). No nodes to move it: everything is set in "Object behavior" (Platform character, Ladder). A panel shows the animation playing. Collect the 5 gems.

⬇️ Download the project (156 KB)

Candy match

A Candy Crush-style match-3 game: swap two neighbouring candies to line up 3 or more, chain combos and reach 1500 points in 25 moves. The whole game fits in a few grid nodes.

⬇️ Download the project (238 KB)

Pirate battle

A sea battle with explosions everywhere: steer your ship with the joystick and blast the pirates charging at you and firing back. 3 lives, a score, and they keep getting faster.

⬇️ Download the project (121 KB)

Platformer

A 3-screen platformer: coins to collect, enemies to stomp by jumping on them, spikes, 3 lives and a finish flag.

⬇️ Download the project (144 KB)

Neon shooter

A neon-style arcade ship shooter: blast the waves of enemies and push your score up.

Neon jump

A neon platformer with a camera that follows the player, traps and an exit portal.

Maze chase

A tile maze: collect every coin while two flies chase you, finding their own way around the walls.

⬇️ Download the project (121 KB)

Lost on the moon

A tense choice-based story: an astronaut lost on the Moon. The dialogues are available in 9 languages.

Effects showcase

A showcase of every visual effect: explosions, fire, rain, snow, electrocution, flash, fade… with a single tap.

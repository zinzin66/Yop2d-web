Yop2D

The first 100% no-code 2D game engine, designed and built exclusively for Android tablets.

Native and Standalone Creation

Develop your ideas directly on your tablet. Yop2D runs natively in Java and embeds its own binary Manifest customization system. This means you can export your creations as fully standalone APK files, without ever needing a PC or writing a single line of code.

Engine Capabilities

Logic and Events

The Blueprint system intelligently manages your scenes. You can trigger actions on every frame, handle complex collisions, and listen to zone entries or exits.

Movement and AI

Bring your entities to life. The engine offers dedicated action nodes allowing an object to automatically chase or flee a target.

Advanced Physics

Enable physics in one click. Apply impulses, add force by angle, and modify the bounce or gravity of your objects in real time.

Appearance and Animations

Master the visual rendering. Play with opacity, change the display order (Z-Order), generate mirror effects, and set up powerful parallax effects.

Inventory and Progression

Create deep games using variables. Add or remove items, check for their presence in the inventory, and manage complete save points (checkpoints).

Dialogues and Interface

Communicate with the player. Display interactive dialogues, dynamically modify texts, and manage the opening or closing of the HUD depending on the situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Yop2D?

Yop2D is a 2D game engine exclusive to Android. It allows anyone to create a complete video game through a visual interface (Blueprint), without any programming knowledge.

Can I create complex mechanics?

Yes. Although it is no-code, Yop2D includes advanced features divided into categories: spatial logic, time management (stopwatches and timers), and mathematical operations.

How do I publish my game?

Yop2D is totally standalone. The engine compiles your project and generates an APK file that you can share, install, or publish, all directly from your tablet.

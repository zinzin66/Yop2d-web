Projects & Export

From the home screen, find your projects with their thumbnails and open the example games to learn quickly. With a single button, Yop2D builds your game into a standalone APK file, directly on your device: no computer, no server, no third-party tool.

Visual Level Editor

An interface inspired by professional engines, designed for touch screens. Tap an object to select it, drag to scroll the scene, and use the handles to move, resize or rotate it. Hide or lock any object with one tap so nothing gets moved by accident.

Tile-Based Levels

Import a tileset, paint your levels tile by tile, and mark once which tiles are solid. Collisions are handled automatically, so platformers and top-down maps are quick to build.

Real-Time Animation Editor

See your imported frames at a glance and preview your 2D animations in real time, right inside the engine. In game, animations follow pause and slow motion like everything else.

Built-in Titles & Text Effects

Create stylized titles (gradients, outlines, neon, shadows, relief, textures, curves) directly in the engine, instead of making heavy text images in other apps. Your final APK stays light.

Visual Node System

No code to write: you build your game logic with visual blocks (nodes) connected together. 127 ready-to-use nodes cover movement, physics, animation, sound, dialogues, inventory, camera, timers and conditions. Collapse whole chains of nodes to keep your workspace clear.

The Brain of Your Games

Select a node to set it up: pick its target object, adjust values, choose images, sounds or animations from lists. Simple formulas like =score + 1 let you use variables, the finger position, the joystick, time or object properties anywhere.

Example games included

Five example games come with the engine: Neon shooter (an arcade space shooter), Neon jump (a platformer with a following camera, traps and a portal), Lost on the moon (a choice-based story in 9 languages), an effects showcase that plays every visual effect at the touch of a button, and a parallax scrolling scene. Open them, play them, then change them to understand how everything works.

On Tablets and Phones

Tablets offer the most comfortable experience, but Yop2D also runs on Android phones in landscape mode. On small screens, the panels switch to a compact layout to leave as much room as possible for your scene.

Engine Capabilities

No-code visual nodes

127 nodes to build your game logic by connecting blocks, with simple formulas when you need them.

Offline APK export

Your game becomes a real Android app, built on the tablet or phone itself, even without internet.

Scenes as objects (prefabs)

Any scene can be placed as an object inside another scene: perfect for a row of identical enemies or reusable pieces.

Tag targeting

Give objects a tag, and one node acts on all of them at once: move every enemy, destroy every coin.

Physics & behaviors

Gravity, bouncing, impulses, collisions, ground detection, plus chase and flee behaviors for enemy AI.

Tilemaps

Paint levels from a tileset and mark solid tiles once: collisions are automatic.

Variables, inventory & saving

Score, lives, inventory lists, and saving or restoring the game state at any time.

Touch controls

Virtual joysticks, buttons and HUD overlays, with multi-touch: move and jump at the same time.

Pause & slow motion

The whole game follows pause and slow motion, while your pause menu keeps working.

Dialogue, sound and camera

Text dialogue, sound effects with volume control, looping music, a camera that follows the player within the level limits, camera shake and vibration.

Particles and visual effects

13 particle presets (explosion, fire, smoke, rain, snow, hyperspace, shockwave…), trails, glow, electrocution, hit stop, flash, tint, vignette and screen fade.

Games in several languages

Write each dialogue in several languages: the game automatically shows the language of the player's phone, or the one chosen in a menu.

Yop2D is made for you if…

  • you only have an Android tablet or phone, and no computer;
  • you want to create anywhere, even without internet;
  • you don't want to learn a programming language;
  • you want a free tool with no ads, no subscription and no watermark in your games;
  • you want to share your games as Android apps with friends, family or students.

What Yop2D does not do (yet)

  • Your games run on Android only: no iPhone, web or computer version.
  • No AAB export yet, the format required to publish on Google Play. It is planned for a later version.
  • 2D only.
  • It is a young engine, in testing: the community and the number of tutorials are still small, and bugs are possible.
  • No scripting language for experienced programmers: everything is done with nodes.
  • The engine is free, but its source code is not public.

When another engine is a better choice

  • You need iPhone, web or computer versions of your game: GDevelop, Construct or Godot.
  • You want to learn real programming or make 3D games: Godot.
  • You work on a computer and want a no-code engine with a large community: GDevelop or Construct.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Yop2D?

Yop2D is a free, no-code 2D game engine for Android. It lets anyone create a complete video game on a tablet or phone with visual nodes, without programming knowledge, and export it as an APK directly on the device.

Is Yop2D really free? Are there ads?

Yes, Yop2D is 100% free: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no hidden fees. There are no ads in Yop2D, and there never will be.

Who owns the games I make? Can I sell them?

Your games belong to you. You can keep them, give them away, share them or sell them, without asking permission and without paying anything to Yop2D.

Is there a Yop2D logo or watermark in my games?

No. The games you export contain no Yop2D logo, splash screen or watermark.

Is Yop2D open source?

No. Yop2D is free to use, but its source code is not public. This changes nothing for your games: they are entirely yours.

I don't know how to code. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Yop2D replaces code with visual nodes that you connect together. It is made for beginners and for creators who prefer to focus on their game. The beginner guides show you how, step by step.

Which devices does Yop2D run on?

Android tablets and phones with Android 7.0 or newer. Tablets are the most comfortable; on phones, Yop2D works in landscape mode with a compact interface. There is no version for iPhone, iPad or computer.

Do I need a computer or an internet connection?

No. Yop2D creates and exports your game as a standalone APK directly on your device, 100% offline. No cloud build, no account.

Can I publish my game on Google Play?

Not directly yet. Yop2D exports APK files: you can install them, share them, or publish them on sites that accept APKs, such as itch.io. Google Play requires the AAB format signed with your own key: this export is planned for a later version.

Can I play my Yop2D game on iPhone, on the web or on a computer?

No. Games made with Yop2D are Android apps. If you need other platforms, see the comparison for engines that support them.

What kinds of games can I make?

2D games: platformers, top-down adventures, shooters, arcade and puzzle games, quizzes, interactive stories… The tile editor, physics, collisions, animations, dialogues, inventory and saving cover most needs. Yop2D does not make 3D games.

Can I use my own images, sounds and music?

Yes. You import your own images, tilesets, animation frames, sound effects and music. Titles and text effects are created directly in the engine.

What languages is Yop2D available in?

The interface, the built-in help, the guides and the node reference are available in 9 languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.

How do I learn to use Yop2D?

Start with the three example games included in the engine, then follow the beginner guides. The node reference explains all 127 nodes, and the YouTube channel, Discord and Telegram are there to help.

Why does Android show a warning when I install the APK?

Because the APK is downloaded outside the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. You can also join the closed test on Google Play: how to become a tester.

Does Yop2D collect data?

Yop2D sends anonymous usage statistics: when it is opened and closed, how long it was used, and crash reports (type of error only), with the app version, Android version and device language. The country is estimated from the connection, without storing the IP address. No personal data and nothing from your projects. You can turn it off on the home screen, and the games you export send nothing. Details: privacy policy.

Is Yop2D finished?

Yop2D is in active development and in closed testing on Google Play. It is already usable to make complete games, but bugs are possible: export your projects regularly as a backup, and report problems on Discord or Telegram.

Who makes Yop2D?

Yop2D is made by an independent developer, with the feedback of its community of testers.

How is Yop2D different from GDevelop, Construct or Godot?

Yop2D is designed to work entirely on an Android tablet or phone, without code, without internet and without a computer, and it stays free with no ads. In return, it only makes 2D games for Android. See the detailed comparison.

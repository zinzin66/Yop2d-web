Getting help from an AI

Beginner guide · no code

Français · English

You can ask an AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) for help when you are stuck in Yop2D. But an AI does not know Yop2D well: on its own, it sometimes invents nodes that do not exist. This guide shows you how to give it what it needs to answer correctly.

👉 An AI can be wrong, even when well prepared. Always test what it tells you. And never paste personal information: what you write is sent to the AI service you use.

Step 1: give it the right file

Yop2D has a file written for AIs: it explains how the engine works, the most common mistakes, and lists every node with its exact name. Its address is:

https://zinzin66.github.io/Yop2d-web/llms-full.txt

  1. If your AI can open links, start your message with: "Read https://zinzin66.github.io/Yop2d-web/llms-full.txt first".
  2. If it cannot (many cannot): open the link in your browser, select all the text, copy it and paste it at the start of the conversation. The file is long: that is normal.

Step 2: write a good message

The more precise your message, the better the answer. Here is a template to adapt:

"Read https://zinzin66.github.io/Yop2d-web/llms-full.txt first. I am making a game with Yop2D (version 1.0.xxxx, I am a beginner, on a phone). What I want to do: … What happens: … What I already tried: … Answer in simple steps, with the exact names of Yop2D buttons and nodes, no code. If you are not sure a node exists, say so."

  • Your version: it is written on the start screen. Some nodes are only in the test version.
  • One problem per message: an AI answers small questions better.
  • Say what you wanted, and what happens instead: it helps the most.

Step 3: show it your nodes

Describing nodes with words is hard. There is an easier way:

  1. Open the Blueprint of the scene concerned.
  2. Tap the { } button (top right): it shows your nodes as text.
  3. Copy this text and paste it into your conversation with the AI, with your question.
  4. If needed, add a screenshot of the node's settings window (AIs that can read images use it well).

Step 4: check the answer

  1. Follow the steps one by one and test after each step.
  2. If the AI mentions a node or setting you cannot find: ask for its exact name, then look for it in the node list. It may be wrong, or the node may only exist in the test version.
  3. Tap ☰ → Check my game: Yop2D lists what is missing (variable, object, file, node connected to nothing).
  4. Still stuck? Tell the AI what you did and what happened, and it can correct itself.

Step 5: ask others

If it still does not work, ask on the Yop2D Discord: give your version, what you wanted to do and, if possible, your exported project (start screen → your project → Export). Your question also helps improve the guides and the file for AIs.

How do I make an object a child of another?

Beginner guide · no code

Français · English · Español · Deutsch · Italiano · Português · Русский · 中文 · 日本語 · العربية

What is a child?

A child is an object attached to a parent: move the parent and the child follows. Handy for text on a button, a weapon in a hand, a shadow under a character.

The top bar

The top bar is the same on tablet and phone (on phone only the icons are shown): ＋ Object, Assets, Save, Undo, Redo, Copy, Delete, Move, View ▾ (hand, grid, center, zoom), Blueprint, Menu and Play.

Method 1: with the Inspector

  1. Select the object that will be the child.
  2. Open the Inspector (arrow at the top right if it is closed), Advanced section.
  3. Tap the Parent line and pick the parent object in the list.

Method 2: by dragging

  1. Open the Hierarchy (left panel).
  2. Press and hold the child object until it lifts off.
  3. Drop it on the parent object: the parent gets a green outline and the child appears below it with the └ symbol.

Detach a child

Long-press the child, then drop it on the Scene line or on a folder. It keeps its place on screen.

Good to know

Both methods give the same result: the Inspector and the Hierarchy always agree. A child's coordinates are counted from its parent's top-left corner. Two objects cannot be each other's parent.

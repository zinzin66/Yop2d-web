The start screen in pictures

Beginner guide · no code

This is the first screen you see when you open Yop2D: from here you create, open, export or delete your projects.

The screen at a glance

  1. 1
    The logo and the moving nodes: just decoration. Tap a node and it sends a little burst of light.
  2. 2
    Three buttons: School mode, Yop2D settings and Language (explained below).
  3. 3
    Check for updates: looks for a new version of Yop2D; the button turns orange when there is one. If you installed Yop2D from Google Play, it is not there: Google Play handles updates.
  4. 4
    Discord and Telegram: to join the community, ask your questions and show your games.
  5. 5
    The version of Yop2D. This is the number to give when you report a problem.
  6. 6
    The three tabs: PROJECTS (your games), EXAMPLES (complete games to open and see how they are made; they download the first time you tap them) and TEMPLATES (small projects that show one single thing, with step-by-step guides). Examples and Templates open full screen.
  7. 7
    The project toolbar (explained below). On the right, the number of projects.
  8. 8
    The project list: tap a project to choose it (it is highlighted). You see its name and the date it was last changed.
  9. 9
    The bottom buttons act on the chosen project. Edit opens it in the editor. Rename changes its name. Duplicate makes a copy, so you can try things safely. Export saves it into a .zip file (a backup, or to send to someone). Build APK builds the game's Android app (an .apk file). Delete erases it, after asking you to confirm.

The small buttons

  • Create a project
    Creates a new empty project. You choose its name and its format (landscape or portrait).
  • Open a downloaded project
    Adds a project you received or downloaded (a .zip file) to your list.
  • Project log
    Shows the chosen project's log: the game's messages, useful to understand a problem. You can copy it to send to someone who is helping you.
  • Projects folder test
    Checks that Yop2D can save its projects. You only need it if you are asked to when something goes wrong.
  • Getting started
    Reopens the panel of step-by-step guides (your first game, your animated hero, the editor…).
  • School mode
    A simplified interface and gentler content, for children and classrooms.
  • Yop2D settings
    The anonymous statistics (which you can turn off), the language, automatic saving and school mode.
  • Language
    Changes Yop2D's language.

Creating a sound effect

Beginner guide · no code

Français · English · Español · Deutsch · Italiano · Português · Русский · 中文 · 日本語

A laser, a jump, a coin pickup… With the sound effect editor, you build a sound with sliders, or you pick an existing sound and tweak it, without leaving Yop2D. It needs Android 10 or later.

Open the editor

  1. In the Assets window, tap the Sound FX button. (You also find it when you choose a node's sound: Sound FX button, then Use this sound effect.)
  2. On the left, a Search a sound… field and the list of sounds sorted in groups. Tapping a sound picks it and plays it.
  3. On the right you see the shape of the sound and its settings.

The adjustable retro sounds

The My adjustable retro sounds group holds 9 starter sounds: Laser, Jump, Coin, Explosion, Power-up, Hit, Click, Alarm, Teleport. Choose the Sound shape (Soft, Square, Saw, Triangle, Noise) then move the 7 sliders: Pitch, Slide, Length, Vibrato, Grit, Echo, Note jump. The sound plays again when you lift your finger.

The Random and Vary a little buttons try sounds for you, and Undo goes back.

The recorded sounds

Under the retro sounds, the ⬇ Download … more sounds line downloads a pack of 435 royalty-free (CC0) sounds: impacts, footsteps, objects, interface, voices… It weighs 3.5 MB, is downloaded only once and needs internet. These sounds have simpler settings: Pitch, Speed, Echo, Volume, Trim the start, Trim the end.

Save and use

  1. Tap Create the sound (.ogg), give it a name and choose the Folder (Sons by default).
  2. The sound effect is now an ordinary sound file: use the “Play sound” node and pick it.
  3. To change it later: Open in the editor.
👉 When you tweak a sound from the pack, its license (CC0) is copied into your project along with it.

Explaining your nodes with notes and frames

Beginner guide · no code

Français · English · Español · Deutsch · Italiano · Português · Русский · 中文 · 日本語

A script of nodes quickly gets long. Notes work like comments: you write, for yourself or for people who open your game, what a group of nodes is for. They change nothing in the game. There are two kinds: the Sticky note (free, anywhere) and the Frame (which surrounds nodes).

The Sticky note

  1. In the Blueprint, tap the sticky-note button (a small folded square) in the top bar: a note appears.
  2. Tap it to edit it: give it a title (optional), write your explanation, pick one of the 6 colors, then tap OK.
  3. Drag it to move it. Pull the corner handle to make it bigger.
  4. Tap the small – to fold it into an i icon. Tap the icon to open it again.
👉 To delete a note, tap it then Remove.

The Frame

  1. Tap the selection button (the dotted square) in the top bar.
  2. Pick the nodes: drag a rectangle in the empty space around them, or tap them one by one.
  3. In the bottom bar, tap Frame them.
  4. Tap the frame's title bar to give it a title and a color.
👉 When you drag a frame's bar, the nodes inside follow it.

The "?" on a frame

Every frame has a small ? that opens a bubble with its explanation. In the bubble, Edit changes the text, Remove removes the frame (the nodes stay) and Close closes the bubble. Perfect for an example or a template that explains itself.

Good to know

  • Undo and Redo work for notes too.
  • Notes are saved with the Blueprint (the 💾 button).
  • "Tidy up the nodes" does not move frames: tidy first, frame afterwards.
  • An older version of Yop2D that reopens and saves your project loses the notes: update Yop2D before sharing a project.
